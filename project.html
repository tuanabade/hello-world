ARCHITECTURE PORTFOLIO 2026

Selected Works

Art Gallery Project developed in Building II Studio.

This art gallery project was developed as part of the Building II Design Studio and explores the relationship between exhibition spaces, circulation, and spatial experience.

The design is organized around a sequence of interconnected gallery spaces that guide visitors through different layers of visual and spatial engagement.

Natural light, structural rhythm, and carefully framed views play a significant role in shaping the atmosphere of the building.

Through the balance of openness and enclosure, the design encourages exploration, reflection and interaction.
